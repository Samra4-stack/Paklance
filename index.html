How It Works

Pakistan's trust problem. Solved.

For years, Pakistani freelancers and businesses burned each other — no verification, no escrow, no recourse. Paklance fixes every single failure point.

For Buyers
1
Browse CNIC-Verified Sellers
Every seller completed a live video verification. Search by category, city, price, and rating. Read verified reviews from real buyers who actually paid.
2
Choose a Package — Prices Are Fixed
Every seller lists Basic, Standard, and Premium packages with exact PKR prices and explicit deliverables. No negotiation. No scope confusion. You know what you're getting.
3
Pay via JazzCash Escrow — Not to the Seller
Your payment goes into a secure escrow — NOT directly to the seller. They only receive it when you approve. You cannot be scammed with advance payment.
4
Approve Delivery — Funds Released
Happy with the work? Approve and escrow releases. Have a problem? Our mediation team resolves it within 72 hours with full documentation.
For Sellers
1
Register & Complete CNIC Verification
10-minute video call with your national ID. Demonstrate real work on screen. Get your Verified badge — the signal that makes local SMEs 3× more likely to hire you.
2
Set Your Packages at Your Real Rate
List Basic, Standard, and Premium at your actual market rate. No more haggling. No more undervaluing. Transparent pricing attracts serious clients and filters time-wasters.
3
Receive Orders — Always Get Paid
Buyers only contact you after payment is in escrow. You will always be paid for work you genuinely deliver. "I'll pay after you send the file" is impossible here.
4
Receive via JazzCash — Only 10%
Half of Fiverr's 20%. Payment released to your JazzCash or Easypaisa immediately after buyer approval. No bank account needed. Just your mobile.
